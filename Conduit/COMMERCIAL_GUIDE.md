## When You're Ready to Sell

### Step 1: Choose Business Structure
**Options:**
- **Sole Proprietorship** (easiest, you already are one)
  - Just report income on personal taxes
  - No formation paperwork
  - Full personal liability
  
- **LLC** (recommended for selling)
  - File with your state (~$100-500)
  - Liability protection
  - Professional appearance
  - Separate bank account

### Step 2: Pricing Models
**Options for Conduit:**
- **Freemium** - Free version + paid "Pro" features
- **One-time purchase** - $X for lifetime license
- **Subscription** - $X/month for updates + support
- **Pay-what-you-want** - Let users choose

### Step 3: Distribution
**Where to sell:**
- **VS Code Marketplace** (free to publish, millions of users)
- **Gumroad** (easy digital sales)
- **Your own website** (keep 100% of revenue)
- **GitHub Sponsors** (recurring support)

### Step 4: Update License (when selling)
If you form an LLC later, just update:
```
Copyright (c) 2025 Conduit LLC
```
